LoanBook — Delete your account

Developer: Gaurav Kumar · App: LoanBook

This page explains how to permanently delete your LoanBook account and the data associated with it. LoanBook is a personal record-keeping app for money you have lent or borrowed and the interest on it.

Option A — Delete from inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open the LoanBook app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.
  5. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option B — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address registered on your account and we will delete it for you:

  • Send an email to gauravkumar.cs1988@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line “LoanBook account deletion request”.
  • Include the email address or mobile number registered on your account so we can verify ownership.

We process verified requests within 7 days, and in all cases within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all of the following:

DataWhat happens
Account profile — name, mobile number, email, password, languageDeleted
People you added (names, numbers, notes)Deleted
Loan records — principal, interest, dates, status, notesDeleted
Payments and transactionsDeleted
Subscription recordDeleted
Email verification / password-reset codesDeleted
Chat messages you sent or received, and your conversation listDeleted
Your membership of any chat groups Deleted — groups you created remain for their other members
Notification tokens for your devicesDeleted
Approximate location, if you allowed itDeleted

Because a chat message is stored once and shared by both people in the conversation, deleting your account also removes those messages from the other person's chat history.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your loan history and balances cannot be recovered afterwards. If you want a copy of your records first, use Profile → Backup my data (PDF) in the app before deleting.

What data is kept, and for how long

We do not retain a copy of your personal or financial records after deletion. Limited transactional logs (for example, server access logs that may incidentally contain an IP address) may persist for up to 90 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, after which they are automatically discarded. We never sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

See also our Privacy Policy.
